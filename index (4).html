From proof of concept to real retailer monitoring

An incremental deployment plan based on the current manual process described by Shelly.

01

Controlled MAP catalog

Upload approved style-level minimum advertised prices and any valid promotional exceptions.

02

Retailer price collection

Use approved retailer feeds, monitoring vendors or permitted public-page retrieval with reliable product matching.

03

Comparison & evidence

Compare observations with the applicable MAP and preserve verifiable listing URL, timestamp and captured evidence.

04

Internal review & trends

Route potential deviations to a human reviewer, track resolution and report coverage and recurring issues.

Suggested four-week pilot

  • Week 1: Obtain 20 bestselling styles, controlled MAP list and 10 participating retailers
  • Week 2: Validate product matches and reliable price capture
  • Week 3: Schedule checks, exception matching and evidence retention
  • Week 4: Audit accuracy, false-positive rate and staff time saved

What this demonstration proves

  • Single source of truth for sample MAP prices
  • Repeatable price comparison across retailer/product combinations
  • Exception-aware alerts with a review queue
  • CSV-based integration and export to support workflows
  • Live demo interaction: simulate a price drop, scan, resolve
Compliance boundary: Monitoring is not enforcement. Canadian resale price-maintenance rules and any contracts/policies require qualified legal/commercial review. This prototype never sends notices or directs retailers to change prices.

Suggested interview positioning
